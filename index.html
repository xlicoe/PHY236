General Information

Welcome to PHY 236: Advanced Lab I. This is a writing-emphasis laboratory course where students carry out 10 experiments, including an independent lab project. The course covers experiments in modern physics such as atomic spectra, radioactivity, and the photoelectric effect.

Prerequisites: Highly recommended to concurrent enrollment in PHY-235: Modern Physics

Lab Reports

Lab reports must be written clearly, concisely, and formatted professionally following standard structure for physics publications. We will practise using LaTeX via Overleaf for typesetting your reports.

Report Structure

  • Abstract: Summary of experiment, methodology, and primary result.
  • Introduction & Theory: Physical principles and governing equations.
  • Experimental Setup: Schematic and description of the apparatus.
  • Data & Analysis: Data presentation and detailed error analysis.
  • Conclusion: Interpretation of results.

Overleaf (LaTeX) Quick Start Guide
